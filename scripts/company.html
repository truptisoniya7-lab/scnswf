About us

Suresh Chandra Nayak Social Welfare Foundation (SCNSWF) is a grassroots non-profit organization dedicated to creating lasting social impact across underserved communities. Our focus lies in improving access to healthcare, education, and essential support services for children, women, the elderly, and marginalized groups. Driven by compassion and guided by purpose, we work hand-in-hand with local partners and volunteers to build a healthier, more equitable society. At SCNSWF, we believe in action that uplifts, empowers, and transforms lives.
